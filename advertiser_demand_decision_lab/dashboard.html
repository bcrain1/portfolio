A decision aid with explicit limits
This is a deliberately constructed observational simulation, not a completed A/B test, demand forecast or proof of advertising experience. It omits campaign budgets, media supply, seasonality, repeat attempts, auction dynamics and revenue. A launch is an operational milestone, not advertiser satisfaction or incremental value.
Before prioritizing a real intervention, validate instrumentation, inspect friction reasons with users, estimate implementation effort, and prospectively define an evaluation with guardrails. Do not turn a scenario score into an ROI promise.
