# Role fit and next improvements

This independent example was scoped against [Netflix Analytics Engineer 5 Demand Science, JR42808](https://explore.jobs.netflix.net/careers/job/790318658684), whose verified description emphasizes investigations, metric ownership, reliable analytical data, stakeholder recommendations and planning/campaign tools. The project is not affiliated with Netflix and uses no employer data.

The useful proof is an end-to-end analytical decision: define the unit and metric contract, inspect data loss, distinguish composition from change, expose assumptions, and explain when to investigate or withhold a recommendation. Python/SQL and a usable dashboard make the reasoning inspectable. It does not establish advertising employment experience, production Hadoop/Spark, ML deployment or past business impact.

The improvements already implemented over the original content lab are the distinct advertiser-attempt schema and business question, baseline/current cohorts, exact mix-shift fixture, recovery sensitivity and break-even, missingness hold, empty state, conflict-failure regressions and precomputed browser views. These are more useful than adding an unnecessary model purely for appearance.

The next worthwhile improvements, if the owner chooses to extend it, are a reviewed campaign-complexity and repeat-attempt model, explicit intervention costs with a common terminal outcome, and a prospective validation design. Those should follow domain and data-contract review. An LLM, forecast or optimizer is not needed to explain the current decision and would not cure missing identification or real instrumentation evidence.
