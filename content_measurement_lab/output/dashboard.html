Metric contract and limitations
Unit / anchor
One synthetic member. Eligibility is known before exposure and anchors both groups.
Exposure
A valid live exposure in the first hour. Missing exposure telemetry may misclassify a member.
Maturity
All displayed member rates require a full 168 hours of event-time follow-up. Late ingestion can still revise the result.
Engagement
At least 600 observed heartbeat seconds in one live session starting during the first 24 hours; restricted to exposed members in the funnel.
Watch time
Unique valid heartbeats, capped at 60 seconds each. Unreported viewing is not imputed.
Session boundary
Explicit play, content change, or over 30-minute gap within member/device. Orphan heartbeat sessions are retained but do not count as starts.
Uncertainty
Wilson intervals assume independent-member binomial sampling. They do not correct measurement error, selection bias, or household dependence.
Scope
Local SQLite demonstration. CSV fixtures; no production scale, cloud warehouse, or distributed-execution claim. AI-assisted implementation, with source and tests available for review.
